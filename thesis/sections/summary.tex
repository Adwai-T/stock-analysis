\section{Summary of Work}

In this project I have tried to reseach the Indian stock market behavior and tried to build a stock analysis system that would work for in accordance with the Indian stocks. This project focuses on building forecasting models that are specifically designed to understand and learn patterns from the Indian stock market.

For this I have tried to retrieve the most recent data for the Indain companies. To limit the scope of the project and make it comparable I chose NSE as the exchange and equity as the point of focus.

I used Kite connect API for it affordable price and generous limits on usage to get the most recent 2 years of data for all equities listed on NSE. This data was then used to train  models. It is also used to get day to day data so that it could be added to the existing data of any stock in consideration and then train stock specific models on them.

And Finally a working demonstration of the complete project is created in python and the dashboard for visualization of the results is created in HTML and JS without using any farmwork.

\section{Problem Statement and Solution}

Problem Statement

\begin{itemize}
  \item Create a stock market forecasting application, by training models on data available form Indian companies and compare the results with traditional statistical and manual technical analysis tools.
  \item Then validate these models on on their accuracy, generalizable, and scalining ability, to find the best models possible for each usecase.
\end{itemize}

Gaps addressed by the Project

\begin{itemize}
  \item Study and compare models on India specific dataset.
  \item Compare multiple different types of time series and hybrid models.
  \item Evaluate models on different size of data sets.
  \item Consider the impact of Indicators on the overall models performance
  \item Implement a demo to showcase the use of these models in trading.
\end{itemize}

\section{Findings and thier Significance}

\begin{itemize}
  \item GRU models worked best when they were trained on limited data, mostly considering single company's data without using any indicators.
  \item This estabilished the ability of GRU models to specalize well but they lack when it comes to generalizing well to large datasets.
  \item LSTM models showed the ability to train well on larger and smaller datasets, making them extremly versatile. They out performed most GRU, transformers and CNN LSTM in most cases.
  \item Transfomer models even though gave good results when were trained on engineered features and large datasets, still did not give significantly high improvement over LSTM model trained on the same data.
  \item CNN-LSTM models out performed the LSTM models in very large data sets and when used with indicators but lacked with smaller datasets.
  \item CNN-LSTM models were exceptional in understanding local patterns as well as remembering the long term temporal patterns.
  \item Feature Engineering with indicators proved useful in enhancing the patterns when it was used with larger datasets.
  \item Indicators did not make a large difference when were considered with Raw features to train smaller models with GRU and LSTM especailly for single company data.
  \item NIFTY50 made of larger compnaies that are mostly stable and do not have extreme volatility and thus models trained with them did not generalize as well to the overall market patterns.
  \item NIFTY200 is composed of a diverse set of industries and companies and hence models trained with them got a large enought dataset that captured the overall market patterns much better.
  \item Models trained with all companies data performed well and generalized to the overall market behaviour but lacked as compared to specialized models trained on that companies data.
\end{itemize}

\section{Limitations of the Project}

\begin{itemize}
  \item To limit the scope of the project limited data set was considered.
  \item Only daily data for past two years was considered.
  \item This project does not go into training for specific industries.
  \item Technincal indicators were kept constant and were not selected based on models or industries for optimal training.
  \item The models were only trained to predict next days closing and not trends.
  \item The models do not take into consideration any fundamental data or sentimental data.
  \item The project only focused on showing the prediction, but did not connect it to any automation system for re-enforcement learning
\end{itemize}
